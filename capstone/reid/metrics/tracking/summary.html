Experiment conditions

Identical teacher checkpoint and raw candidate cache. baseline_motion disables appearance. McByte variants use upstream association, camera-motion compensation and cached vehicle embeddings instead of the person ReID network. mcbyte_no_masks disables temporal mask cues; mcbyte_edgetam enables the released EdgeTAM. All exported masks come from the teacher, not propagated masks. McByte can recover candidates above 0.1 confidence; baseline uses its original 0.3 threshold. McByte export IDs are finalized after the sequence, as in its upstream demo. This is an adapted system, not a reproduction of published sports benchmark scores.

timing.csv separates replay cost from cached teacher time. Their sum is an estimate and cannot be claimed as live pipeline latency. Startup and final file export are excluded.
