Suggested research bar

What would make a strong thesis result?

There is no universal publication cutoff. These are proposed ReID-branch goals for the new full-frame model. A complete result also needs detection AP, instance-mask AP, tracking association metrics, and end-to-end frame latency. Predeclare a fresh identity holdout before the final experiment.

1 · Retrieval benefit

+2 points mAP over matched ReID-only on a fresh cross-camera holdout. Repeat with at least 3 training seeds and report a paired confidence interval for the difference.

2 · Real masks

≥70% foreground IoU on at least 200 aerial vehicle crops from unseen scenes. Report small vehicles separately. This is a project goal, not an official benchmark record.

3 · Efficiency

Faster than two models at comparable retrieval accuracy: measure joint inference against a separate segmenter + ReID pipeline on the same hardware and input size. Count detection and tracking for any full-system claim.

The local VRU and VRAI results have already been inspected during development. They are useful for improvement, but a final claim needs untouched identities. VRU mAP equals mean reciprocal rank because the gallery has one positive per identity.
