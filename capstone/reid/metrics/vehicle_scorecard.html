What would make your model a viable small alternative?

Identity quality

Beat your own ReID-only arm

On the identical holdout, improve Rank-1 and mAP. Demonstrate foreground IoU on at least 200 held-out aerial vehicle crops; 70% is your current project goal.

Complete video quality

Competitive HOTA + AssA

Use one fixed KITTI car validation split for all reproduced models. Show that good HOTA is not hiding poor association.

Deployment efficiency

Lower measured ms/frame

Beat a separate detector + segmenter + ReID pipeline on the same hardware and resolution while maintaining comparable quality. Include preprocessing, postprocessing, and tracking.

No published universal cutoff exists. A model can be viable by matching larger systems at substantially lower latency, memory, or power. Report parameters, peak memory, power, mask mAP, and box mAP alongside the tables above. No fair published latency number can replace running the baselines on your own hardware.
